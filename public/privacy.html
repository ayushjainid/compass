Privacy policy

Your data stays yours.

Effective October 5, 2026 · what changed

In short: Compass uses your sign-in (Google, or your email and a password) only to know it's you. Everything you enter is stored under your account, readable only by you, and never sold, shared for advertising, or used to train AI. You can export it or delete it at any time.

Who runs Compass

Compass (life-compass.web.app) is an independent personal project. In this policy, "we" means the developer who runs it. Contact details are at the end.

What we collect

  • If you sign up with email: your email address, the first name you give (optional) and a password. The password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication, stored only as a secure hash, and never visible to us. Firebase sends the emails for confirming your address and resetting your password.
  • From Google sign-in: your name, email address, profile photo and a Google account identifier. Compass requests only Google's basic sign-in permissions (openid, email and profile). It cannot read your Gmail, Drive, Calendar, contacts or anything else in your Google account.
  • What you enter: your goal, chosen values, components and floors, daily and weekly check-ins, journal lines, weekly reviews, names you add to the People list, and settings such as your colour theme.
  • If you allow notifications for reminders: your device's push address (a web address issued by your browser's push service, such as Apple, Google or Mozilla), your time zone, the reminder times you choose, the date you last opened Compass, and whether this week's review is done. Nothing from your entries is included.
  • If you send feedback: the note you write, your browser type and the screen you were on.
  • If something goes wrong in the app: a short list of recent error messages from your devices (the error text, where in the app it happened, your browser type and the time), at most 20 kept. It never includes what you've entered.
  • If you turn on the live calendar: a long random link that lets your calendar app fetch your plan (floor names, days and times; never notes, journal or tasks). Anyone you give the link to can see that plan, so treat it like a private address. You can replace or turn off the link at any time in Compass, which makes the old one stop working.
  • Reminder buttons: the evening reminder can name what's still open today and offer buttons to tick one or two of them. Those names then show on your lock screen; turn off Say what's left in Compass → Reminders to keep them off.
  • Anonymous usage counts: the first time each day and each week that you open Compass, log a floor, plan, review, add a task, finish setup, turn on reminders, pause a floor, export your calendar or send feedback, Compass adds 1 to a shared tally for that day or week. For weekly opens it also notes how many weeks ago you finished setup (0 to 12+), so we can see whether Compass keeps being useful. The tallies are plain numbers: they contain no account identifier, name, email or anything you've entered, and they can't be traced back to you. Your own settings remember which tallies you've already added to that day, so you're counted once.
  • If you import from other apps: files you add during setup (from Todoist, TickTick, Sunsama, Outlook or a calendar) are read on your device and never uploaded. If you connect Microsoft To Do, Compass asks Microsoft for read-only access to your tasks (the Tasks.Read permission), reads your lists once in your browser and then discards the access. Only what you choose to keep, such as your routines, open tasks and people, is saved to your account.

We do not collect your location, contacts, device identifiers or browsing activity, and Compass loads no third-party analytics or advertising code.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your account separate from everyone else's.
  • To store your entries and sync them across the devices where you sign in.
  • To read your feedback and fix errors.
  • To see, in total, how many people use Compass and which parts help, from the anonymous tallies above.
  • To show you your own progress, trends and suggestions. Suggestions are calculated in the app from your own data.
  • If you allow notifications, to send at most one evening check-in a day and a Sunday review reminder. Turning reminders off, or deleting your account, removes the push address.

We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, use it for profiling, or use it to train artificial intelligence models. We do not read your entries.

Google user data

Compass's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Information from Google sign-in is used only to provide sign-in and to show your name and photo inside the app.

Where it's stored and how it's protected

  • Data is stored with Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore) in the United States. Google encrypts it in transit and at rest.
  • Database rules allow each signed-in account to read and write only its own data. No other user can access it.
  • A copy is kept in your browser so Compass works offline. Signing out removes Compass's saved copy of your entries from that browser; to remove everything on a shared device, also clear the site's data in your browser settings.

Sharing

We share data only with Google, as the service provider that hosts Compass and runs sign-in and storage, and, if you turn on reminders, with Cloudflare, which runs the small scheduler that sends them, and with your browser's push service, which delivers them. Reminder messages are encrypted so that only your device can read them. We share only what is needed to operate the app. We would disclose information if required by law.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Your data is kept for as long as you have an account.
  • Export: in the app, open the Compass tab and choose Export under Your data.
  • Delete: open the account menu (your photo, top right) and choose Delete my account. This permanently removes your entries and your Compass sign-in account right away. Compass keeps no backups of your data.

Children

Compass is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
