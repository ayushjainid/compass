Privacy policy

Your data stays yours.

Effective September 27, 2026

In short: Compass uses your Google sign-in only to know it's you. Everything you enter is stored under your account, readable only by you, and never sold, shared for advertising, or used to train AI. You can export it or delete it at any time.

Who runs Compass

Compass (life-compass.web.app) is an independent personal project. In this policy, "we" means the developer who runs it. Contact details are at the end.

What we collect

  • From Google sign-in: your name, email address, profile photo and a Google account identifier. Compass requests only Google's basic sign-in permissions (openid, email and profile). It cannot read your Gmail, Drive, Calendar, contacts or anything else in your Google account.
  • What you enter: your goal, chosen values, components and floors, daily and weekly check-ins, journal lines, weekly reviews, names you add to the People list, and settings such as your colour theme.

We do not collect your location, contacts, device identifiers or browsing activity, and Compass loads no analytics or advertising code.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your account separate from everyone else's.
  • To store your entries and sync them across the devices where you sign in.
  • To show you your own progress, trends and suggestions. Suggestions are calculated in the app from your own data.

We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, use it for profiling, or use it to train artificial intelligence models. We do not read your entries.

Google user data

Compass's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Information from Google sign-in is used only to provide sign-in and to show your name and photo inside the app.

Where it's stored and how it's protected

  • Data is stored with Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore) in the United States. Google encrypts it in transit and at rest.
  • Database rules allow each signed-in account to read and write only its own data. No other user can access it.
  • A copy is kept in your browser so Compass works offline. Signing out removes Compass's saved copy of your entries from that browser; to remove everything on a shared device, also clear the site's data in your browser settings.

Sharing

We share data only with Google, as the service provider that hosts Compass and runs sign-in and storage, and only as needed to operate the app. We would disclose information if required by law.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Your data is kept for as long as you have an account.
  • Export: in the app, open the Compass tab and choose Export under Your data.
  • Delete: open the account menu (your photo, top right) and choose Delete my account. This permanently removes your entries and your Compass sign-in account right away. Compass keeps no backups of your data.

Children

Compass is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. Significant changes will be noted in the app.
